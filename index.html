COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before visiting The Opulent Beauty Bar.

While we welcome walk-ins based on chair availability, we strongly recommend booking in advance—especially for long treatments like Nanoplastia, Balayage, HydraFacials, and weekend appointments—to ensure zero wait time in our VIP suites.

Nanoplastia is an organic, formaldehyde-free amino acid treatment that penetrates the hair cortex to heal and straighten without harsh fumes or tears. Keratin primarily coats the hair shaft for frizz reduction. Our senior stylists offer free strand tests to advise which one suits your hair best.

We adhere to strict hospital-grade hygiene: all stainless steel cuticle pushers, nippers, and bits are autoclaved and sealed in individual sterile pouches opened right in front of you. Foot tubs feature single-use disposable protective liners.

We are conveniently situated at Shop No. 10/1, Latif Plaza, Block 6, Gulshan-e-Iqbal, Karachi. Dedicated parking assistance is available, and we are easily accessible from University Road and Rashid Minhas Road.
